Membarongkan

Membarongkan depicts an exaggerated face of a young girl, morphed into the likeness of a barong. Culturally, the barong is a symbol of protection to ward off evil spirits much like the evil eye in the west. To most, it's red face, bulging eyes and bared sharp teeth gives off the impression of an angry person. This work was created with the intention of warding off the "evil" of anger. The title "Membarongkan" is a wordplay on the slang "membagongkan", a word used to describe something suprising, upsetting and confusing.
Oil pastels on A5 paper.
